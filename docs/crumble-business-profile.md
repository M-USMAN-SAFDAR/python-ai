# Crumble Pakistan — independent student case study

The student's reference to "Crumbles" is interpreted as **Crumble Pakistan**, the cookie brand. This is a public-information exercise, not a commissioned project or company endorsement.

## Verified public information

- Crumble Pakistan is a cookie business. Its own story describes its start on university premises and subsequent bakery business: https://crumblepakistan.com/pages/our-story
- The company's Lahore launch announcement names Johar Town and MM Alam: https://www.linkedin.com/posts/crumblepakistan_crumble-enters-lahore-1482025-after-a-activity-7360313381750218752-5vZL
- Sources checked on 16 October 2026. No current prices, hours or delivery promises are required for this exercise.

## Inputs used for this assignment

```text
Business name: Crumble Pakistan
Business type: cookie bakery
City: Lahore
Main customers: university students and families
Monthly budget (PKR): 15000
```

The city is the case-study location, not a claim that it is the headquarters. The audience is an assumed campaign audience, not a verified demographic profile. The 15000 PKR budget is an assumed exercise budget, not company spending. The late-order complaint is hypothetical, not an allegation about this business.

No business representative supplied details, reviewed the tool or approved the output. If the professor requires direct work with an owner, that requirement still needs to be fulfilled.
